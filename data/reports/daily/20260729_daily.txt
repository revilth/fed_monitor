1. DISSENTS — "FAMILY FIGHT" FRAMING
   "I asked for a good family fight, and I got one. Most of our discussions were

   Beyond statement: Warsh explicitly embraced the three hawkish dissents as evidence

2. BOND MARKET — HIGHER YIELDS WELCOMED, NOT FEARED
   "I think it's a good thing if the bond market moves based on economic data as
   opposed to the Fed's policy." Warsh noted "nominal and real yields are materially
   higher across the Treasury curve" since the previous meeting and framed this as
   confirmation of committee credibility, not as a tightening-of-financial-conditions
   headwind.

   Beyond statement: The opening statement does not reference the yield curve. In Q&A,
   Warsh explicitly cited 10y at 4.67% and 30y at 5.21% (19-year high) as positive
   signals. This is the inversion of the pre-Warsh posture, where long-rate spikes
   would have triggered a financial-conditions caveat. Warsh treats bond-market
   skepticism as validation, not warning. [Quote reconstructed from CNBC recap —
   verify framing against PDF.]

3. CREDIBILITY SELF-ASSESSMENT — PERSONAL MILESTONE
   "Markets broadly saying this FOMC has credibility to deliver on dual mandate."
   "Feel better about FOMC's ability to deliver on inflation target than I did initially."

   Beyond statement: The opening carries the institutional commitment. The Q&A adds
   Warsh's personal progress assessment — two meetings in, he is explicitly saying the
   credibility-building exercise is working. "Feel better... than I did initially"
   is a Chair's first-person milestone declaration, not a data reading. Combined with
   the "new chapter" framing, this is a Chair consolidating the June 17 turning point,
   not hedging it.

4. HIKE CONDITIONALITY — NO-HESITATION LANGUAGE
   "I want to stress, of course, that decisions by this committee matter a great deal,
   and where necessary and appropriate, we will not hesitate to act."

   Beyond statement: The statement holds rates with no directional guidance. The Q&A
   adds an implicit hike threat — "will not hesitate to act" is Warsh's personal
   statement of willingness to tighten if warranted. Consistent with the three dissents
   and with Waller's still-standing July 13 conditionality (the June CPI kept Waller
   in the majority; a hot August CPI print could tip him).

5. COMMUNICATIONS PHILOSOPHY — OPACITY AS VIRTUE
   "Market participants are learning to play the ball, not the referee — and market
   prices will continue to respond in the direction and magnitude they see fit. This
   is, in my view, a change for the better."

   Beyond statement: This is Warsh's articulation of why a less communicative Fed is
   more credible. Forward guidance absence is not a transitional awkwardness; it is
   a structural improvement. This is the live-fire operationalization of the framework
   announced at June 17's task forces.

NET Q&A SIGNAL: HAWKISH — "no-hesitation," family-fight normalization, and
higher-yields-as-validation each reinforce the committee's tightening bias without

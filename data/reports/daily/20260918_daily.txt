SPEECH 1 — "Initial Findings from Independent Review of Silicon Valley Bank"
Classification: Type B — supervisory reform announcement.
Content: Bowman announced initial findings of the independent Starling Advisory
  Group review of SVB's March 2023 collapse. Seven key findings: supervisory staff
  knew of SVB vulnerabilities as early as March 2022 but did not take timely action;
  root cause was a culture of risk aversion and unclear decision rights, NOT
  regulatory tailoring or direction from the former Vice Chair for Supervision;
  social media did NOT trigger or accelerate the bank run (Charles River Associates
  analysis). Announced remedial actions already underway: Supervisory Operating
  Principles statement; examiners to submit monthly escalation reports to leadership.
Policy signal: none. Regulatory significance: high (first formal independent
  accountability assessment of SVB; frames Bowman's supervisory reform agenda).

SPEECH 2 — "The Final Chapter on Modernizing Bank Regulatory Stress Testing"
Classification: Type B — stress testing framework finalization.
Content: Previewed the upcoming final rules on enhanced transparency and public
  accountability for the bank stress capital buffer framework. Key elements:
  publication of stress test model equations, coefficients, assumptions, and
  scenario-design process; reduction of year-over-year capital requirement
  volatility; introduction of forward-looking stress exercises (results would NOT
  affect capital requirements — informational/supervisory only). Basel III capital
  reforms and G-SIB surcharge revisions expected to be finalized by year-end 2026.
Policy signal: none. Regulatory significance: high (completes the stress-testing
  modernization program Bowman began on confirmation as VCS).

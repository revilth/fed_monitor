  reason to hold.
  Direction: hawkish lean

LABOR MARKET
  "the labor market is solid" — upgraded from Sept 1's "stable."
  Three consecutive upgrades in Barr's labor arc: March 26 ("stable but
  vulnerable to shocks") → Sept 1 ("stable, no qualifier") → Sept 23
  ("solid"). Matches Warsh's post-hike framing ("consistent with full
  employment") and Barkin's Sept 22 ("good grades"). No data cited; the
  brevity is consistent with a short embedded section.
  Direction: hawkish lean

INFLATION
  "inflation is above our 2 percent target and not clearly trending
  toward target in a timely way" — the "timelier" rationale from the
  September 16 statement, rendered in negative form (not trending
  timeward). Explicit risk-asymmetry claim: "risks to achieving our
  inflation target have increased, while risks to the labor market have
  receded." Duration language ("for over five years") absent, probably
  due to the shorter format.
  ⚠ ATTRIBUTION DIVERGENCE: Barr retains his shock-attribution framing
  ("tariffs, the conflict in the Middle East ... surge in investment
  demand to support AI buildout") even though the September 16 statement
  DELETED the supply-shock attribution clause that had been present in
  every prior statement since at least June 2025. This is the first
  documented divergence from the committee's updated language. Williams
  has also consistently defended attribution (see CALIBRATION.md); a
  two-governor attribution bloc (Barr + Williams) persisting inside a
  committee that officially dropped it is an analytical flag.
  Direction: hawkish lean

MONETARY POLICY
  "The FOMC took important action to that end last week by increasing
  the policy rate, which I supported." — explicit personal endorsement,
  first person. Rationale: "We needed to recalibrate monetary policy to
  reflect the balance of risks" and "we were out of position." Path:
  "In my base case, further policy adjustments are likely to be needed
  to ensure inflation comes down to target in a timely fashion." This
  is a meaningful forward signal — the base case is further tightening.
  Cut signal: absent. Hike signal: BASE-CASE, further moves likely.
  Direction: hawkish

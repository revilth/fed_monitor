Chicago Fed President and CEO Austan Goolsbee will participate in a moderated Q&A at the The Economy Ahead: A Luncheon with the Chicago Fed, an Illinois Manufacturers’ Association event occurring on September 29 in Champaign, Illinois. The discussion, moderated by Kara Demirjian Huss, president and founder of DCC Marketing and senior vice president of TCCI Manufacturing, will livestream from this webpage at 12 pm CT.
